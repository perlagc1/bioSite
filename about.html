Early Life

Faustino was born and raised in Guanajuato, Mexico, one of ten children. His childhood taught him the value of hard work and family.

Journey to the United States

At fourteen, Faustino came to the U.S. to help his family. His dedication and work ethic shaped his life and inspired those around him.

Family and Legacy

Married for nearly twenty years, Faustino continues to work hard for his wife and children, creating memories through travel, family gatherings, and love.
