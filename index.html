My Husband, My Role Model

Faustino Gómez was born and raised in Guanajuato, Mexico, one of ten children. He grew up in a humble home filled with love, responsibility, and hard work. At fourteen, he came to the United States to help his family, working in fields, ranches, turkey farms, and eventually Tyson. His dedication, kindness, and discipline shaped the life he built for his wife and children.
